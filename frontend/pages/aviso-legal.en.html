Data sources and thanks

Cardmática's card and set catalogue is built from YGOPRODeck, our main source, and Yugipedia. Each card page keeps the details of where its data came from.

Thank you to the teams and communities who keep these databases open: without their work, this catalogue would not exist.

Yugipedia texts are used under that wiki's licence. Neither YGOPRODeck nor Yugipedia is affiliated with Cardmática.
