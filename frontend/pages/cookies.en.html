A cookie is a small file the site stores in your browser. Cardmática uses only necessary cookies: without them you cannot sign in and your account cannot be protected. We use no advertising, statistics or third-party cookies, so there is nothing to accept or refuse; the cookie notice only informs.

Cookies we use

CookieWhat forLifetime
cm_sessionKeeps you signed in. The browser does not let page scripts read it.12 hours, or 30 days if you choose to stay signed in
cm_csrfSecurity: stops another site from making changes to your account on your behalf.Same as the session
cm_langRemembers the language you chose.1 year

The session and security cookies exist only if you sign in and are removed when you sign out.

Data kept in your browser

The site also keeps in your browser's storage (these are not cookies and are not sent to the server) your preferences and what you do without an account: cm_theme (light or dark mode), cm_cur (currency), cm_market (TCG or OCG), cm_view (list or grid), cm_wantlists_v1 and cm_profile_v1 (your lists and profile without an account), cm_wizard (shopping assistant options), cm_promo_closed (set banners you closed) and cm_cookies_seen (that you have read the cookie notice).

How to delete them

You can delete the site's cookies and data from your browser settings. If you delete the session cookies you will have to sign in again; if you clear the storage you will lose the lists and preferences that are not in an account.
