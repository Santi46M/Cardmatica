What Cardmática is

Cardmática is a catalogue of Yu-Gi-Oh! TCG and OCG cards for buyers and sellers in the Mercosur. Today it lets you look up cards, printings, banlists and price history, and save wantlists and a profile. Real buying and selling is not available yet: the products, sellers and prices in the tabs marked «demo» are illustrative.

Catalogue content

Card data comes from public sources cited on each card page, with its provenance. It may contain mistakes; if you find one, let us know. Card images are shown only when there is permission to do so.

Buying and selling (once available)

Cardmática will put buyers and sellers in touch; each sale will be an agreement between them. The terms for payment, shipping, returns and disputes will be published here before sales open.
