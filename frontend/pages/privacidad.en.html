Privacy policy

Draft pending legal review. This text describes how the site works today and is not a substitute for a lawyer's advice. The final version will be published before real buying and selling opens.

Who is responsible

The controller of your data is the operator named in the legal notice, which also gives the email address for exercising your rights.

What data we keep and why

  • Account: email address, display name, the date you accepted the terms and confirmed being 18 or older, and the password stored as a hash (nobody can read it). They are used to sign in and to tell you about changes to your account.
  • Sessions: start date, last activity and the browser used, so you can see and end your open sessions.
  • Profile, if you fill it in: name and country; the store and payment methods only if you sell; address and phone are optional and will be used only for shipping once it exists. We do not store card numbers or bank details.
  • Wantlists and preferences: the cards you are after, your price alerts, currency, market, language and catalogue view.

We do not ask for data the site does not need for what it does today. Optional fields say so.

Minors

Cardmática is for people aged 18 or over and does not knowingly collect minors' data. If we find an account belonging to a minor, we delete it with its data.

In your browser

When you are not signed in, the profile, wantlists, preferences and the demo cart are kept only in your browser. The cookies we use are all necessary and are described in the cookie policy. We use no advertising or tracking cookies.

Usage statistics

We count how often each section of the site is viewed and a few actions (opening a card page, searching, creating an account, adding to a wantlist), added up per day. Those counts carry no cookies, no IP address and nothing that identifies you. If your browser asks not to be tracked (Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control), we count nothing.

Third parties

Your browser connects only to Cardmática: we load no scripts, fonts, statistics or advertising from other companies. We do not sell or share your data with third parties for commercial purposes. Account emails go out through the mail server the operator configures.

How long we keep it

For as long as you keep the account. Ended or expired sessions and used email links are removed automatically. Deleting the account removes everything above.

Your rights

You can see and correct your data in your profile, download a copy or delete your account in «Security and sign-in», and write to us about anything else. The laws that apply depend on your country: Law 18.331 in Uruguay, Law 25.326 in Argentina, the LGPD (Law 13.709) in Brazil; you can also complain to your country's data protection authority (URCDP, Agencia de Acceso a la Información Pública or ANPD).
