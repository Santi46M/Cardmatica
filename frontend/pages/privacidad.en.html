Privacy policy

Draft pending legal review. This text describes how the site works today and is not a substitute for a lawyer's advice. The final version will be published before real buying and selling opens.

What data we keep

  • Account: email address, display name and the hashed password (never the password itself).
  • Sessions: start date, last activity and the browser used, so you can see and end your sessions.
  • Profile, if you fill it in: name, store, country, address, phone and accepted payment methods. We do not store card numbers or bank details.
  • Wantlists and preferences: the cards you are looking for, your price alerts, currency, market, language and catalogue view.

In your browser

When you are not signed in, the profile, wantlists, preferences and the demo cart are kept only in your browser. When you are signed in, we use two necessary cookies: one to keep you signed in and a security one against forged requests. A third cookie keeps the language you choose. We use no advertising or tracking cookies.

Usage statistics

We count how often each section of the site is viewed and a few actions (opening a card page, searching, creating an account, adding to a wantlist), added up per day. Those counts carry no cookies, no IP address and nothing that identifies you. If your browser asks not to be tracked (Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control), we count nothing.

What we use it for

Only to make the site work: signing in, saving your lists and, once buying and selling exists, handling orders and shipping. We do not sell or share your data with third parties for commercial purposes.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, correct it or delete your account. The laws that apply depend on your country (for example, Law 18.331 in Uruguay, Law 25.326 in Argentina, the LGPD in Brazil); the final version of this policy will name the controller and how to exercise them.
