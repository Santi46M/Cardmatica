Today

Real buying and selling is not open yet: the demo store charges nothing, so there are no purchases to return and no money to refund. This page explains how it will work once sales open.

Withdrawal button

When you buy at a distance on Cardmática you will be able to withdraw without giving reasons, at no cost and without signing in, from the «Withdrawal button» link at the foot of every page. We will send you a code identifying your withdrawal request within 24 hours. The time limits depend on your country:

  • Argentina: 10 calendar days from the contract or from receiving the product, whichever is later (Law 24.240, art. 34; Civil and Commercial Code, arts. 1110 ff.; Resolution 424/2020).
  • Brazil: 7 days from receiving the product, with a refund of everything paid, shipping included (Consumer Protection Code, art. 49; Decree 7.962/2013).
  • Uruguay: 5 business days from the contract or the delivery of the product (Law 17.250, art. 16).
  • Paraguay and Bolivia: the time limits will be published after the legal review; meanwhile, at least what your country's law says applies.

Problems with a product

If a card does not match its listing (another printing, language, edition or a worse condition) or arrives damaged, you will be able to complain to the seller from your order and, if it is not solved, to Cardmática. This does not replace your country's legal warranty.

Refunds

Refunds will be made to the same payment method, for the full amount paid. Who pays for return shipping depends on your country's law and will be stated in each case before you return anything.
