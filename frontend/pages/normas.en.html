Community rules

Draft pending legal review. This text describes how the site works today and is not a substitute for a lawyer's advice. The final version will be published before real buying and selling opens.

  • Treat buyers and sellers with respect.
  • Describe cards honestly: their real printing, language, edition and condition.
  • Selling counterfeits, proxies or altered cards as if they were originals is not allowed.
  • Do not use the site to send advertising, scam or impersonate someone else.
  • Do not try to get into other people's accounts or get around the site's security limits.
  • If you see something that breaks these rules, let us know.
